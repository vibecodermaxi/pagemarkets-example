# pagemarkets-example

<!-- slot:pagemarkets.line.1 -->
An example of a GitHub repo with parts for sale on [PageMarkets](https://pagemarkets.com). Whoever buys a part owns it and decides what it says, and our GitHub App commits their words to this repo.
<!-- /slot -->

Your project could be here: one line, a link and a small logo, seen by everyone who opens this repo.

## What is for sale here

Six parts, one or two of each kind a repo can sell:

- **The line above**, at the top of this README. Everyone who opens the repo sees it.
- **The line under *Sponsors***, further down this README.
- **[The guide](docs/guide.md)**, a whole page of its own.
- **The banner on [the example site](https://vibecodermaxi.github.io/pagemarkets-example/)**, a block of the page its owner can style.
- **The site's colours**, in [style.css](style.css).
- **The greeting on the example site**, set by a few lines of code in [greeting.js](greeting.js).

Each part has a tier, from 1 (words, links and pictures only) to 4 (code), and a buyer sees it before they buy.

## How it works

1. Someone buys a part on PageMarkets.
2. They write in it. Our App commits their words to this repo, usually within a minute, as a Verified commit.
3. If anyone changes the part back, the App puts the owner's words back.

The owner can sell it on or rent it out by the day. Someone else can also buy it from them without asking, for more than it's worth at the time, and the owner is paid when that happens.
